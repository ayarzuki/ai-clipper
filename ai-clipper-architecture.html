AI Clipper (Shorts) — System Design

Long video → 9:16 shorts for Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts — Groq Whisper + DeepSeek V4.1 Flash ≈ $0.04 per source hour

Stage guards

  • • ASR chunked at 10 min (25MB limit), 3s overlap deduped
  • • Retry ×3 backoff on 429 (Groq free-tier limits)
  • • LLM output validated: clamp bounds, snap to sentence edges
  • • Drop clips <15s or overlapping >30%

LLM contract (JSON)

  • • {start, end, title, hook, caption, hashtags, virality_score}
  • • 20–59s target · 6–12 clips/hour · no overlaps
  • • hook ≤7 words → burned on first 3 seconds
  • • >4h video → map-reduce (per-hour pass → rank)

Render spec

  • • 1080×1920 @30fps, H.264 CRF20, AAC 128k
  • • ASS captions: 2–4 words/cue, karaoke highlight
  • • MarginV≈520 (TikTok safe zone: bottom 484px, right 86px)
  • • Already-vertical source → skip crop
